FAQ

Common questions.

What is NeuroSense?

NeuroSense is a concept for an AI-based wearable and alert platform focused on monitoring signals associated with stress and sensory overload.

Who is it designed for?

It is intended to support caregivers who need broader visibility into a child’s physiological and environmental patterns.

How does the alert system work?

When sensor readings exceed or shift beyond predefined patterns, relevant information is delivered to authorized caregivers for faster response.
