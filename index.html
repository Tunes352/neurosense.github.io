NeuroSense is an AI-based wearable monitoring concept designed to continuously observe physiological and environmental signals and provide timely information to caregivers when patterns associated with stress or sensory overload are detected.

Core Capabilities

One wearable. Multiple signals. Smarter support.

NeuroSense combines physiological and contextual measurements to provide a broader picture rather than relying on one signal.

♥

Physiological Monitoring

Monitors heart rate, HRV-related measures, skin conductance, temperature and SpO₂ for continuous observation.

◌

Stress & Overload Detection

AI-assisted analysis can combine multiple signals to identify patterns that may indicate elevated stress or sensory overload.

🔔

Emergency Alerts

When configured thresholds are reached, the connected system can notify caregivers so they can provide appropriate support.

⌁

Activity Awareness

Motion information provides context around changes in physiological signals and physical activity.

☀

Environmental Monitoring

Light and environmental sound measurements can provide additional sensory context.

🔒

Privacy Focused

The system concept emphasizes secure data handling and collecting only information necessary for the intended purpose.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NeuroSense diagnose autism?

No. NeuroSense is a prototype wearable monitoring concept. Physiological signals cannot independently diagnose autism, anxiety or a particular emotional state.

Why use multiple sensors?

Different physiological and environmental signals can provide complementary information and may provide better context than using a single measurement.

Can NeuroSense automatically intervene?

The current concept focuses on monitoring and caregiver notification. Any intervention should be designed with caregivers and qualified professionals.

What are the next development steps?

Future development could include hardware prototyping, sensor calibration, personalized baseline collection, AI model validation, usability testing and security review.
